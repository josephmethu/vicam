PROJECT ENQUIRIES

How your brief is used.

When you send a project brief, VICAM receives your name, email, selected service and project description, along with any optional contact or site details you include.

Purpose and access

VICAM uses these details to understand your request, follow up and manage a possible project. Staff who manage enquiries can view the brief, add private follow-up notes and link it to a client profile. If work proceeds, the client workspace may hold project details, tasks, service agreements, quotations, invoices, payment references and support requests. Payment references record payments already received; this website does not collect payments. A company email notification may alert staff to a new brief; it does not contain the full client details.

Share only what is needed

Do not include passwords, banking account details, confidential case files, student records or other sensitive personal information. The education dashboard is a demonstration and this enquiry form is not a student-data collection system.

Client portal

Invited client contacts use a separate sign-in account. Authorized contacts can view information shared with their client organization, download shared PDFs and files, submit support requests, exchange messages and record quotation decisions. Team notes, internal project descriptions and assignments are not displayed in the client portal. Invitation links allow account setup or password reset; keep them private. Contact VICAM if a recipient should lose access.

Uploaded requirements and handover files are stored in the project database and included in backups. Do not use this portal for student records, credentials, banking details or confidential case material. Quotation approval records the account, saved document reference and time of the response. The portal uses an essential sign-in cookie, and client pages are not cached.

Retention and requests

Retention should reflect the purpose of the enquiry and any resulting engagement. Use VICAM’s published contact details to ask about access, correction or deletion of your enquiry.

Device storage

The website stores your light or dark appearance preference on your device. The staff workspace uses a sign-in cookie. Saving a brief downloads a local text file; sending a brief stores it in VICAM’s enquiry inbox.
