Follow-ups needing attention

New enquiries waiting at least two calendar days, tasks due today, quotations awaiting a response, overdue invoice balances and contracts ending within 30 days. These reminders appear in the dashboard; they do not send emails.

Private client access

Verify the recipient’s identity and email before sending an invitation. Each account can see the selected client’s shared records.

Open client sign-in

Send the generated link privately. It expires after 24 hours and works once. Email is queued only when selected, with configured SMTP and an encryption key. A reset invalidates existing sessions when used.

Client-visible project update

Publish a concise update. Internal project descriptions and staff assignments stay private.

Share a quotation or invoice

Create the original PDF in Client records first. Quotations must be sent or accepted, and invoices issued. Sharing grants the selected client access to that exact copy.

Share requirements and handover files

PDF, PNG, JPG or text · 2 MB per file · 50 MB per client. Files are stored in the database and included in backups. Do not upload student records, credentials or sensitive case files.
