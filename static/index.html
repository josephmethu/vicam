A few answers to help you plan your work with VICAM.

How do I start a project?

Use Discuss a project to share your goals and contact details. You will receive a reference after submission. VICAM will review the brief and follow up to clarify the scope.

What information should I provide?

Explain the problem, the outcome you want and any timing needs. Site details help with CCTV; current systems and team size help with IT support. An outline is enough to begin.

Can I see progress and documents online?

Once VICAM creates your client account, a private invitation lets you set a password. The client portal shows shared updates, quotations, invoices, files and support conversations.

How do I approve a quotation?

Review the original PDF and its terms in your portal. You can accept a current quotation or request changes. Your response is recorded, and VICAM will confirm the next steps.

How do support and response times work?

Submit a support request through your portal and follow its status and conversation there. Support scope, working hours and response commitments are agreed in your service contract.

Should I send student data or passwords?

No. Share a broad project outline. Student-data systems need a separate agreement and appropriate access controls. This website and client portal are not student-record systems. See the privacy notice.

More of your document workflow in one place.

Clear quotation breakdowns

List quantities and unit prices, with a document-wide discount and VAT rate where appropriate. Review the calculated total before issuing the saved PDF.

SHARE

Private client access

Invite the intended client and expressly share the document in their portal, with access agreed around the information they need to see.

REVIEW

Approval of a specific copy

Let the client approve an eligible shared quotation or request a change. Their recorded decision relates to that copy, keeping later revisions clear.

HOW IT WORKS

One document. A clear way to check it.

01

Issue & share

Your business prepares the quotation or invoice, saves the issued copy and shares its PDF with a QR code or verification link.

02

Open & compare

The client scans the QR code or opens the link to review the saved details. They can also check whether their PDF exactly matches the saved original.

03

Check the latest status

Review quotation validity or the current business-recorded invoice payments and balance. Reload the verification page for a fresh check.

FOR YOUR BUSINESS

Fewer questions about the copy a client received.

For example, a client can compare an emailed quotation with the saved copy before accepting it, or check the remaining invoice balance after your team records a payment. The setup includes document issuing, verification links and an agreed approach to what recipients can see.

BEFORE WE START
What does an exact PDF match mean?

The received file is identical to the issuer’s saved original. A different result means the files differ; the client should ask the issuer to confirm the correct copy.

What do the verification states mean?

Current: saved commercial terms match the current document record. Earlier copy: those terms have changed since it was issued. Withdrawn: the issuer has withdrawn verification. Unavailable: the system cannot verify that copy.

Can clients see when a quotation has expired?

The page shows the validity date and warns when an outstanding sent quotation has expired. It also shows the current recorded status. Clients can contact the issuer for a fresh quotation.

Are payments confirmed by a bank?

No. Payments and balances reflect entries recorded by the issuing business. The verification page does not confirm bank clearance or collect payments.

Who can see the document details?

Access and disclosure are agreed during setup. Anyone holding a verification link can see the details enabled for that link; sensitive PDFs can be shared through the authenticated client portal.

Can the system calculate a quotation total?

Yes. It supports quantities and unit prices, with one document-wide discount and VAT rate. Manual totals remain available. The business reviews the correct tax treatment before issuing the document.

Can a client approve the quotation privately?

An invited client can approve an eligible quotation copy expressly shared in their portal or request a change. This records their account’s decision on that copy; it is separate from certificate signing of a PDF.

Does a QR code prove who issued the document?

The QR code opens the saved verification record. Certificate-based PDF signing is a separate configuration; recipients should check the signer and certificate trust in a compatible PDF reader when signing is enabled.
